iCloud Sync

If data is not syncing seamlessly across your devices, please follow the steps below on each of your devices.

1. Verify iCloud settings

iOS

  • Open iOS Settings app
  • Tap your name (near the top)
  • Verify the Apple ID (name & email) shown is the same across all your devices
  • Tap iCloud
  • Find our application in the "Saved to iCloud" list (formerly "Apps using iCloud") list & verify it is enabled

macOS

  • Open Apple menu ()
  • Choose System Settings
  • Click your name (near the top)
  • Verify the Apple ID (name & email) shown is the same across all your devices
  • Tap iCloud
  • Click "See All" under "Saved to iCloud" (formerly "Show More Apps" under "Apps Using iCloud")
  • Find our application in the applications list & verify it is enabled

2. Check iCloud storage space

Syncing requires that you have enough available iCloud storage space.

3. Update your software

iCloud Sync will work best with the latest software updates. Please make sure all your devices have the latest version of our application installed. Also make sure you are using the latest versions of iOS, iPadOS, and macOS.

4. Restart your devices

Apple Watch Sync

If data is not syncing to your Apple Watch, please follow the steps below.

1. Restart your devices

Restarting both your iPhone & Apple Watch is typically able to resolve any sync issues.

2. Update your software

Apple Watch sync will work best with the latest software updates. Please make sure all your devices have the latest version of our application installed. Also make sure you are using the latest versions of iOS and watchOS.

Widgets

Installation Guides

  • iOS Home Screen: https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT207122
  • iOS Lock Screen: https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT200285
  • macOS: https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT211789

If the widget is not listed as available to be installed, please restart your device and make sure to open the app at least once.

If data is not updating on your widget, please try the steps below:

1. Restart your device

Restarting your device is typically able to resolve any widget issues.

2. Update your software

Widgets will work best with the latest software updates. Please make sure all your devices have the latest version of our application installed. Also make sure you are using the latest versions of iOS, iPadOS, and macOS.
